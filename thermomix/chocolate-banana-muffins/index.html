Chocolate Banana Muffins

Built to use up a bunch of bananas. Soft, very banana-y muffins with dark chocolate chunks and peanut butter.

Makes
12 muffins
Ready in
47 min
Oven
180°C / 350°F
On the Thermomix
4 steps

Thermomix program

  1. 3 Chop 3 sec speed 7
  2. 10 Mix 20 sec speed 4
  3. 15 Mix 10 sec ↺ speed 3
  4. 17 Mix 5 sec ↺ speed 2

Ingredients

0 of 11

Method

0 of 19

  1. More

    Bake on the middle rack.

  2. More

    Using chocolate chips? Skip this step and the next one, and add the chips in step 16.

  3. More

    You want rough chunks, not powder. No need to wipe the mixing bowl.

  4. More

    The riper the better: black-spotted or fully black bananas give the most banana taste and sweetness. Break them into pieces.

  6. More

    Makes them richer and a bit denser. To skip it, use 75 g butter in step 5 instead.

  7. More

    Brown sugar keeps them moist and adds a caramel taste. With very black bananas, 70 g is enough. Sugar-free: Lakanto (Golden is closest to brown sugar) at the same amount, or a 1:1 Truvia baking blend. For other Truvia, check the bag for how much replaces sugar. Pure stevia does not work here: no bulk, and the muffins come out dry.

  15. More

    Stop as soon as there is no dry flour left. Mixing longer makes the muffins tough.

  17. More

    Just enough to spread the chocolate through the batter. Finish with the spatula if needed.

  18. More

    Fill each cup about three quarters full. Press a few chocolate pieces on top if you saved some.

  19. More

    A few moist crumbs on the toothpick is fine. Wet batter is not.

Good to know
  • Keeps 3 days in a closed tin, or freeze them and warm one up for 20 seconds in the microwave.
  • No peanut butter? Use 75 g butter in total.
  • Want them less sweet? The bananas carry a lot of sugar, so 70 g brown sugar is plenty.
