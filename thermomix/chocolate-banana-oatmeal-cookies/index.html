Chocolate Banana Oatmeal Cookies

Two bananas, a bowl of oats and some dark chocolate. No flour, no butter, no added sugar.

Makes
12 cookies
Ready in
36 min
Oven
180°C / 350°F
On the Thermomix
4 steps

Thermomix program

  1. 3 Chop 3 sec speed 7
  2. 5 Mill 10 sec speed 9
  3. 10 Mix 10 sec speed 4
  4. 13 Mix 20 sec ↺ speed 3

Ingredients

0 of 7

Method

0 of 16

  1. More

    Bake on the middle rack.

  2. More

    Using chocolate chips? Skip this step and the next one, and add the chips in step 12.

  3. More

    You want rough chunks, not powder. No need to wipe the mixing bowl.

  5. More

    This makes oat flour. It holds the cookies together, since there is no egg or butter.

  6. More

    Use very ripe bananas with black spots. For a richer cookie, add 30 g peanut butter too.

  13. More

    Reverse keeps the oats and the chocolate chunks whole. If the dough sticks, help it with the spatula through the hole in the lid.

  14. More

    The oats soak up the banana, so the dough is easier to scoop and the cookies come out chewier.

  15. More

    One heaped tablespoon per cookie. Wet fingers stop the dough from sticking. They do not spread in the oven, so the shape you press is the shape you get.

  16. More

    They are soft when hot and firm up as they cool, so do not move them too early.

Good to know
  • Use bananas with black spots. The browner the banana, the sweeter the cookie.
  • Keeps 3 days in a tin, a week in the fridge. Freezes well.
  • Gluten free if you buy oats labeled gluten free.
  • No egg, no butter, no added sugar. Dairy free if your chocolate is.
