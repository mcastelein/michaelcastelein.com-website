Chocolate Banana Oatmeal Cookies

Two bananas, a bowl of oats and some dark chocolate. No flour, no butter, no added sugar.

Makes
12 cookies
Ready in
36 min
Oven
180°C / 350°F
On the Thermomix
4 steps

Thermomix program

  1. 2 Chop 3 sec speed 7
  2. 3 Mill 10 sec speed 9
  3. 4 Mash 10 sec speed 4
  4. 5 Fold 20 sec ↺ speed 3

Ingredients

0 of 6

Method

0 of 8

  1. More

    Use baking paper or a silicone mat. Bake on the middle rack.

  2. More

    Start with 70 g dark chocolate in the mixing bowl. You want rough chunks, not powder. Tip them into a small bowl. No need to wipe the mixing bowl. Using chocolate chips? Skip this step and add them with the oats later.

  3. More

    This turns a third of the oats into flour. The oat flour holds the cookie together, since there is no egg or butter.

  4. More

    That is 250 g banana in pieces, 1 tsp vanilla extract, ½ tsp cinnamon and 1 pinch salt. Scrape down the sides of the mixing bowl with the spatula afterward. For a richer cookie, add 30 g peanut butter here.

  5. More

    Reverse keeps the oats and the chocolate chunks whole. If the dough sticks, help it with the spatula through the hole in the lid.

  6. More

    The oats soak up the banana and the dough firms up. It is easier to scoop, and the cookies come out chewier.

  7. More

    One heaped tablespoon per cookie. Wet fingers stop the dough from sticking. They do not spread in the oven, so the shape you press is the shape you get.

  8. More

    They are done when the edges turn golden. They are soft when hot and firm up as they cool, so do not move them too early.

Good to know
  • Use bananas with black spots. The browner the banana, the sweeter the cookie.
  • Keeps 3 days in a tin, a week in the fridge. Freezes well.
  • Gluten free if you buy oats labeled gluten free.
  • No egg, no butter, no added sugar. Dairy free if your chocolate is.
