Chocolate Banana Oatmeal Cookies

Two bananas, a bowl of oats and some dark chocolate. No flour, no butter, no added sugar.

https://michaelcastelein.com/thermomix/chocolate-banana-oatmeal-cookies/

Makes

12 cookies

Prep

10 min

Bake

16 min

Total

36 min

Ingredients

  • 70 g dark chocolate, in pieces (or chocolate chips)
  • 150 g rolled oats
  • 250 g very ripe bananas, peeled, in pieces (2 large)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pinch salt

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Line a baking tray with baking paper.
  2. Place 70 g dark chocolate in the mixing bowl and chop 3 sec/speed 7. Transfer to a small bowl and set aside.
  3. Place 50 g rolled oats in the mixing bowl and mill 10 sec/speed 9.
  4. Add 250 g bananas, 1 tsp vanilla extract, ½ tsp cinnamon and 1 pinch salt, then mix 10 sec/speed 4. Scrape down the sides of the mixing bowl with the spatula.
  5. Add the remaining 100 g rolled oats and the chopped chocolate, then mix 20 sec/reverse/speed 3, helping with the spatula.
  6. Leave the dough to rest in the mixing bowl for 10 minutes, so the oats soak up the banana.
  7. Scoop 12 heaped tablespoons of dough onto the baking tray and press each one flat to about 1 cm thick. They do not spread in the oven.
  8. Bake for 14 to 16 minutes (180°C) until the edges are golden. Leave to cool on the tray for 10 minutes before you move them.

Tips

  • The browner the banana, the sweeter the cookie. Black-spotted bananas are what you want.
  • If you use chocolate chips, skip step 2 and add them in step 5.
  • For a richer cookie, add 30 g peanut butter with the bananas in step 4.
  • They keep 3 days in a closed tin, or a week in the fridge. They freeze well.
  • Gluten free if you use oats labeled gluten free.
