Suno fill button · updated 2026-10-24 12:13 · 3 songs

1. Install once

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar. You never need to drag it again: it loads the newest songs from this page each time you click it.

Suno fill

No bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B.

2. Use it

  1. Open suno.com/create. Choose Songs, then Advanced.
  2. Click Suno fill in your bookmarks bar. A small menu opens at the top right.
  3. Click a song. It fills Title, Style, Exclude and Lyrics.
  4. Click Create. Do the human check if Suno asks.
  5. Next song: click the next one in the menu. Done songs get a check mark.
