HSK Hits

Live product

Learn Chinese by getting a song stuck in your head.

Mandarin pop songs written for each HSK level, with music videos, lyrics in pinyin and English, and the focus words of every song. The library follows the HSK Standard Course books lesson by lesson. Tap the words you know and the player puts the songs with the most new words first. Songs and videos are AI-made, the site is Next.js and Supabase.

Caffeine Lab

Live, try it

Drag coffees onto a day and watch what your blood does.

A sandbox for understanding caffeine. Drop drinks on a timeline and the curve, the bedtime number and every panel below redraw live. It runs a one-compartment pharmacokinetic model with first-order absorption, so a dose peaks about 45 minutes in and then halves on your own clearance rate. One HTML file, no server, works offline.

Runs locally

Talk, and see your words half a second later. Nothing leaves the machine.

Settled text is black, the working draft is grey and rewrites itself as more words arrive, and a second column translates each line between English and Chinese. Video Search

Runs locally

Type what you remember, get the exact moment in the clip.

Search a phone video library in plain words, like "Lina at the night market", and get back the clip and the timestamp. An AI model describes each video, speech and faces are handled locally, and everything lands in one SQLite index. Matches load straight into a new CapCut project, already trimmed. Works on iCloud and Google Drive, and never moves or deletes an original.
